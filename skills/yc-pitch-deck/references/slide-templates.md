# Slide templates and Demo Day script

Starting points for each core slide. Adapt the words, keep the structure.

## Slide 1, Title
Company name and logo, a one-line description, founder name and contact.
Example: "Stripe for healthcare payments". Do not open with "We are" or jargon.

## Slide 2, Problem
Template: "[Customer type] struggle with [problem]. Today they [painful
workaround]. This costs them [quantified impact]."
Example: "Marketing teams spend 6+ hours a week manually pulling data from five
tools to answer one question: what is working?"
Quantify dollars, time or frequency, and make it visceral. Do not list several
unrelated problems or reach the solution yet.

## Slide 3, Solution
Template: "[Product] is [category] that [key benefit]. Unlike [alternative], we
[unique approach]. The result: [outcome]."
Example: "Databox pulls all your marketing data into one dashboard,
automatically. See what is working in 30 seconds."
Benefit first, no feature list, no technical jargon.

## Slide 4, Why now
Template: "[Recent change] has created [new reality]. This means [customers] now
[new behaviour]. We are positioned to [capture it]."
Sources of timing: technology shift, regulatory change, behaviour change, cost
inflection, platform shift. Do not claim "it is just time" or first-mover
advantage alone, and answer "why not five years ago".

## Slide 5, Market size
Template: "TAM: $X (everyone who could buy). SAM: $Y (who you can reach). SOM:
$Z (what you will win in 3 to 5 years)."
Prefer bottom-up: number of customers times price. Example: TAM $50B all SMB
marketing software, SAM $5B SMBs with marketing teams, SOM $500M marketing teams
using data tools. Avoid "if we capture 1 percent" and top-down numbers with no
logic.

## Slide 6, Product
Screenshots, a 30 to 60 second clip, a before and after, or a live walkthrough.
Show the core experience and what is different. Do not show every feature or use
mock-ups when a real product exists. Pre-product: show the prototype and say so.

## Slide 7, Traction
Template: "$X MRR, growing Y percent month on month. Z customers, A percent
retention. [Timeline context]."
Example: "$30K MRR, growing 25 percent month on month, 100 paying customers at
75 percent monthly retention, started charging four months ago."
Investors weight growth rate over absolute numbers, retention over new users,
revenue over free users, engagement over signups. With no revenue, show waitlist
conversion, letters of intent, pilot commitments or customer testimonials.

## Slide 8, Business model
"We charge $X per [unit] per [period]." Add CAC, LTV, LTV to CAC and payback
when you have them.
Example: "$99 per user per month. LTV $2,400, CAC $400, ratio 6x, payback 4
months."

## Slide 9, Competition
A 2x2 with axes you win on, or a why-we-win list: faster because of a technical
advantage, cheaper because of efficiency, better for a segment because of focus.
Do not dismiss competitors, do not check every box in a feature matrix, and
include the do-nothing option.

## Slide 10, Team
Photo, name, role, previous relevant credential, one line on why they are right
for this problem.
Example: "Jane (CEO) spent 10 years at Google Ads building the SMB product. John
(CTO) is ex-Stripe and scaled payments infrastructure 100x. We lived this
problem daily."
If the team is thin, show advisers, name the early hires, and be honest about the
gap and the plan to fill it.

## Slide 11, Ask
"Raising $X [stage]. Use: [breakdown]. Milestones: [targets]."
Example: "Raising $2M seed. 50 percent engineering to ship v2 and integrations,
30 percent go to market for a first sales hire, 20 percent operations for 12
months of runway. Milestones: $500K ARR, 500 customers, Series A ready."
Do not be vague about use of funds or ask for a wide range.

## Demo Day script, two minutes
- Hook, 10 seconds: "[Company] is [category] for [customer]." Or "[X] companies
  have [problem]. We fix that."
- Problem, 20 seconds: who, what, why it hurts.
- Solution, 20 seconds: what you built, how it works, the key insight.
- Traction, 20 seconds: best metrics, growth rate, proof it works.
- Business model, 15 seconds: how you make money, economics if strong.
- Market and why now, 15 seconds: opportunity and timing.
- Ask, 10 seconds: what you are raising and what it unlocks.
- Close, 10 seconds: a memorable one-liner and a call to action, email visible.

Practice until the flow is natural, pause for impact, memorise the structure not
the words, and finish with energy.

## Quality check
- [ ] 10 to 15 slides, canonical order, no filler slide
- [ ] Each slide carries exactly one point
- [ ] Problem specific and quantified
- [ ] Solution clear within 10 seconds
- [ ] Why now compelling and specific
- [ ] Market size bottom-up and credible
- [ ] Traction is the strongest available metric
- [ ] Competition honest
- [ ] Team backgrounds relevant
- [ ] Ask specific with milestones
- [ ] Readable at presentation distance, consistent formatting, no typos
