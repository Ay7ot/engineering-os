# Marketing Ideas

## When to use
- The team is stuck on how to grow and wants options.
- "What else can we try?", "how do we market this?", "brainstorm marketing".
- Choosing between channels for a given stage, budget and team size.

## Process
1. Read the shared context document (for example `docs/marketing-context.md`, see `product-marketing-context`) before asking anything. Only ask what it does not cover.
2. Establish stage, main growth goal, budget, team size and what has already been tried. Ask about failures too, they narrow the list fastest.
3. Shortlist three to five ideas, not thirty. A long list is a decision the founder still has to make.
4. For each shortlisted idea give: one line on what it is, why it fits their situation, the first two or three implementation steps, what success looks like, and the resources it needs (time, budget, skills).
5. Hand execution to the matching skill rather than doing it here: `cold-email` for outreach, `emails` for lifecycle, `launch` for a release, `seo-audit` and `ai-seo` for search, `page-cro` for the destination page.
6. Say what to ignore and why. Saying no to the wrong channel is as useful as recommending one.

## Suggest by stage
- Pre-launch: waitlist referrals, early access pricing, launch preparation.
- Early: content and SEO, community, founder-led sales.
- Growth: paid acquisition, partnerships, events.
- Scale: brand campaigns, international expansion, media.

## Suggest by budget
- Free: content and SEO, community building, social, comment marketing.
- Low: targeted ads, sponsorships, free tools as lead magnets.
- Medium: events, partnerships, PR.
- High: acquisitions, conferences, brand campaigns.

## Suggest by timeline
- Quick wins: ads, email, social posts.
- Medium term: content, SEO, community.
- Long term: brand, thought leadership, platform effects.

## Output format
For each recommendation: idea name, why it fits (tie it to their context), how to start in two or three steps, expected outcome, and resources needed. Group by quick win, medium term and long term so sequencing is obvious.
