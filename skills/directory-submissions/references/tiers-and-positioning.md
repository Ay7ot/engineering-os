# Directory tiers and positioning variants

## Positioning by directory type

| Surface | Lead with | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Startup directories | Outcome | Audience is other founders; they care what it does |
| SaaS directories | Alternative framing | People search "[competitor] alternative"; meet them there |
| AI directories | AI-first architecture | Their audiences explicitly want AI tools |
| Agent and MCP directories | The agent or MCP angle | Niche but high intent; a real moat |
| No-code directories | Ease plus power | Audience values speed to build over depth |
| Dev directories | Technical depth | Dev audiences reward substance |
| B2B review sites | ROI and use case | Buyers want outcomes and case studies |

## Triage rules

- Submit only where the product is a genuine fit for the category.
- Prefer fewer relevant tiers over volume across irrelevant ones.
- Space batches so listings do not all appear on one day.
- Re-check a listing quarterly; do not amend it weekly.

## Asset variants to prepare

- Tagline: under 10 words.
- Short description: about 60 characters.
- Long description: about 150 words.
- Category tags: 5-8.
- Logo: PNG and SVG, plus a square asset and favicon.
- Screenshots: 5-8 at 1920 by 1080, from the real product.
- Demo video: 60-90 seconds.
- Founder story: 2-3 sentences.
- Vary the opening sentence, feature emphasis and audience framing between tiers.
