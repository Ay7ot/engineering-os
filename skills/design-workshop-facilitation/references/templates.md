# Templates and remote setup

## Problem statement

```text
[user or stakeholder] needs a way to [need or goal] because [insight].
Current state: [what happens today]
Ideal state:   [the outcome we want]
```

Frame problems as opportunities ("how might we ...?") when moving into ideation.

## Customer journey map

```text
Phase: [name]
  Touchpoints: [list]
  Emotions:    [happy | neutral | frustrated]
  Pain points: [list]
  Opportunities: [list]
Repeat per phase.
```

## Ideation shortlist

- Group by theme, then dot vote. Each participant gets three dots.
- Record the rationale next to the shortlist; "we chose it" is not a decision
  record.

## Remote workshop setup

Tools: video conferencing plus a virtual whiteboard that supports sticky notes,
frames and a timer. Everyone remote, or everyone in the room; mixed modes
exclude whoever is on the call alone.

Board preparation:
- One frame or section per activity, with the instructions inside the frame.
- Pre-populate templates: journey map phases, the 2x2 matrix, the agenda.
- Enable the sticky note tool and a visible timer.
- Save the board as a template for reuse.

During the session:
- Everyone on video, breakout rooms for small group work, shared screens for
  context.
- Reaction emojis for quick voting and sentiment.
- Record only with permission, and only for people who cannot attend.

## Facilitation phrases

- Redirect a tangent: "that is worth capturing, let us note it and return to
  where we were".
- Invite a quiet participant: "we have not heard from you yet, what do you
  think?"
- Close a segment: "so the themes are X and Y, and the decision is Z".
- Hand over: "before we finish, who owns each of these and by when?"

## Preparation routine

- Show up ten minutes early to set up the room and test the technology.
- Have the agenda, the templates and the timer ready before anyone joins.
- Keep water and snacks available in person; breaks matter more than a packed
  agenda.
