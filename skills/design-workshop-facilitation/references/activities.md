# Activity scripts by workshop type

Each block lists the activity sequence, timings and deliverables. Use the
timings as a starting point and re-time after the first run.

## Problem definition workshop

Problem statement writing, 15 min.
- Each person writes their understanding of the problem on a separate note.
- Share and cluster similar statements.
- Discuss the differences; they expose assumptions.
- Refine into a single problem statement.

Root cause analysis, 20 min.
- Ask "why" five times to reach a root cause, or draw a fishbone diagram.
- Separate the core problem from its symptoms.

Prioritisation, 15 min.
- Place candidates on a matrix: impact against effort, or urgency against
  importance.
- Dot vote on which to tackle first and record why.

Deliverables: prioritised problem statements, a root cause diagram, a
prioritisation matrix.

## Ideation workshop

Warm-up with crazy eights, 10 min.
- Fold a sheet into eight sections, one idea per section, eight minutes.
- Quantity over quality; the point is to get people sketching.

Silent brainstorming, 10 min.
- Everyone writes ideas individually, one per note, no discussion.
- Explicitly welcome bad and unfeasible ideas; they spark better ones.

Sharing and building, 20 min.
- Each person presents for one to two minutes.
- Others build: "what if we combined this with that?"
- Cluster similar ideas. Build, do not critique, at this stage.

Concept sketching, 15 to 20 min.
- Pick the top three to five ideas and sketch one or two concepts each.
- Focus on how it works, not visual polish.

Deliverables: a full idea list, three to five sketched concepts, grouped themes.

## Customer journey mapping

1. Define the persona, 10 min: one target user, with goals, so everyone maps
   the same journey.
2. Map the phases, 15 min: for example awareness, consideration, purchase,
   onboarding, usage.
3. Identify touchpoints, 20 min: per phase, list every digital and physical
   contact point on the timeline.
4. Map emotion, 15 min: mark each touchpoint happy, neutral or frustrated, and
   draw the emotional line above or below the timeline.
5. Analyse pain points, 20 min: inspect the frustrated points, discuss why, and
   note opportunities.

Deliverables: the journey map, the emotional line, a prioritised pain point
list, an opportunity list per phase.

## Design sprint, five days

| Day | Focus | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | understand and define: map the problem, interview experts, choose the target | target area and success metrics |
| 2 | diverge: sketch individually, crazy eights, then detailed sketches | candidate solutions |
| 3 | decide and storyboard: pick the winner, storyboard the flow | a storyboard |
| 4 | prototype: build the key flows only | a high-fidelity prototype |
| 5 | test: five to seven users, synthesise, decide | findings and next steps |

## Prioritisation matrix

```text
High impact, low effort   quick wins, do these
High impact, high effort  major projects, plan these
Low impact, low effort    fill-ins, do if time
Low impact, high effort   avoid
```
