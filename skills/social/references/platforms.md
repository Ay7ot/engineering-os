# Platform notes and reverse engineering

## Platform strategies

- LinkedIn: B2B primary channel. Cadence 3-5 posts per week; fewer loses momentum and more causes fatigue. Highest-engagement formats in recent years: personal stories with a business lesson, original data or research, contrarian industry takes, and document carousels with 8-12 slides.
- X: indie hacker and developer channel. Build-in-public threads on architecture, revenue and decisions perform well; technical deep-dives are also indexed by search and AI systems, which compounds discovery.
- Instagram: visual brands and lifestyle. Reels and carousels carry reach; stories carry relationship.
- TikTok: broad awareness with younger audiences. Raw and native outperforms polished; trending audio helps distribution.
- Facebook: communities and local business. Groups and native video outperform links.

## Posting and scheduling

- Schedule core content, threads, carousels and evergreen posts.
- Post live for real-time commentary, trend responses and engagement with others.
- Keep 1-2 weeks of content queued, review the queue weekly for relevance, and leave gaps for spontaneous posts.
- Adjust timing from your own performance data rather than generic best-time advice.

## When you are starting out

- Document the journey and share what you are learning.
- Curate and comment on industry content rather than only broadcasting.
- Engage heavily with established accounts before expecting reach.
- When stuck: repurpose old high performers, ask the audience what they want, comment on industry news, or share a lesson from a failure.

## Reverse engineering viral content

Instead of guessing:

1. Find 10-20 accounts with high engagement in the niche.
2. Collect a large sample, at least a few hundred posts.
3. Analyse patterns: hooks, formats, structures and calls to action that recur.
4. Codify the patterns into a written playbook.
5. Layer your own voice on the patterns rather than copying them.
6. Convert the attention into business results with a clear next step.

## Short-form video production

- Length and shape: TikTok 15-60 seconds, Reels 15-30 seconds, Shorts 30-60 seconds, all 9:16 vertical.
- Every video needs three simultaneous hooks in the first second: visual, verbal and on-screen text.
- Captions: at most 2 lines at a time, 3-5 words per line, bold sans-serif with a black outline, key words highlighted, timed exactly to speech. Get a transcript from the editing tool or a speech-to-text pass.
- Structure options: problem and solution (15-30 seconds), list (30-60 seconds), tutorial (30-60 seconds).
- Keep audio clean; poor audio ends retention faster than anything else.
- If a video can be shorter, make it shorter.

## Content ideas by business type

| Business type | Video ideas |
|---|---|
| SaaS | Feature demos showing the outcome first, before and after, "watch me do X in Y seconds" |
| E-commerce | Unboxing, comparisons, how it is made, customer reviews |
| Services | Process reveals, client transformations, myth-busting |
| Personal brand | Lessons learned, contrarian takes, day in the life |

## Hashtags and links

- Hashtags help discovery on Instagram and TikTok; keep them relevant and limited rather than stuffing.
- External links in the body reduce reach on most platforms; place them in comments, the profile, or a follow-up post.
