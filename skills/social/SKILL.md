# Social Content

## When to use
- Creating or planning social posts and a content calendar.
- Repurposing a blog post, podcast, webinar or newsletter into social content.
- Writing hooks, threads, carousels or short-form video scripts.
- Reviewing why reach or engagement is falling.

## Before creating
- Read the project marketing context document (for example `docs/marketing-context.md`) first.
- Goals: the primary objective (awareness, leads, traffic, community) and the action you want from people.
- Audience: who to reach, where they are most active, and what they engage with.
- Voice and resources: tone, topics to avoid, time available weekly, existing content to repurpose, and whether video is possible.

## Platform quick reference
| Platform | Best for | Frequency | Key format |
|---|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn | B2B, thought leadership | 3-5 times per week | Carousels, stories |
| X | Tech, real time, community | 3-10 times per day | Threads, hot takes |
| Instagram | Visual brands, lifestyle | 1-2 posts plus stories daily | Reels, carousels |
| TikTok | Awareness, younger audiences | 1-4 times per day | Short-form video |
| Facebook | Communities, local business | 1-2 times per day | Groups, native video |
- Platform detail and limits: `references/platforms.md`.

## Content pillars
Build around 3-5 pillars. A practical split: industry insight 30 percent, behind the scenes 25 percent, educational 25 percent, personal 15 percent, promotional 5 percent.

For each pillar ask: what unique perspective do you have, what does the audience ask, what has performed before, what can you sustain, and how does it serve the business goal.

## Hooks
The first line decides whether anyone reads the rest.

- Curiosity: "I was wrong about [common belief]." "The real reason [outcome] happens is not what you think."
- Story: "Last week [unexpected thing] happened." "Three years ago I [past state]. Today [current state]."
- Value: "How to [outcome] without [common pain]:" "[Number] [things] that [outcome]:"
- Contrarian: "Unpopular opinion: [statement]" "[Common advice] is wrong. Here is why:"

## Repurposing
The best social content is extracted from existing long-form work, not invented.

- Extract content atoms: a quotable moment (15-60 seconds), a complete story arc (60-90 seconds), a tactical tip (30-60 seconds), a contrarian take, a data callout, or a behind-the-scenes moment.
- A podcast episode usually yields 3-5 short clips, 1-2 text posts, a thread, a carousel summarising the framework, and one newsletter or blog section.
- Workflow: create pillar content, extract 5-10 atoms, adapt each to the platform's format and length, write captions that stand alone, schedule across one or two weeks, and reshare evergreen pieces every 3-6 months. For podcasts, get a transcript, mark the best moments, extract clips, write standalone captions, add subtitles, then spread across a week.

## Calendar and engagement
Plan a week at a time so each pillar appears across platforms. Batch in 2-3 hours: review pillar topics, write the week's posts and threads, draft carousel and video ideas, and schedule everything, leaving gaps for live reactions. Schedule core content, threads and evergreen posts; post live for real-time commentary, trend responses and engagement with others. Keep 1-2 weeks queued and review it weekly.

Daily routine, about 30 minutes: reply to comments on your own posts, comment on 5-10 posts from target accounts, reshare with an added insight, and message 2-3 new connections. A quality comment adds a new insight, shares a related experience, asks a real follow-up, or disagrees with nuance. Build relationships with 20-50 accounts in your space, engage consistently, and credit and share their work before proposing collaboration.

## Short-form video
Short-form video is the highest-reach format on the major platforms.

- The 3-second rule: hit a visual hook, a verbal hook and a text overlay together, all in the first second.
- Problem and solution, 15-30 seconds: hook, agitate, solution, call to action.
- List, 30-60 seconds: "X things that [outcome]", one item every 5-8 seconds, then the call to action.
- Tutorial, 30-60 seconds: show the end result first, then overview, steps, result and call to action.
- Captions: at most 2 lines on screen, 3-5 words per line, bold sans-serif with an outline, key words highlighted, timed to speech. Captions raise watch time substantially.
- Platforms: TikTok 15-60 seconds, Reels 15-30, Shorts 30-60, all 9:16 vertical. Approach detail: `references/platforms.md`.

## Measure and optimise
- Awareness: impressions, reach, follower growth. Engagement: engagement rate, comments (worth more than likes), shares, saves. Conversion: link clicks, profile visits, messages, attributed leads.
- Weekly review: top three posts and why they worked, bottom three and what to learn, follower and engagement trends, and best posting times from data.
- Low engagement: test new hooks, post at different times, try other formats, and engage more with others.
- Falling reach: keep external links out of the post body, post more often, engage in comments, and test video.

## Common mistakes
- Slow hooks that build up to the point, or no call to action.
- No text overlay or captions on video, or poor audio.
- Running longer than necessary.
- Ignoring comments in the first hour.

## Checklist
- [ ] Marketing context read; goal, audience and voice confirmed
- [ ] 3-5 pillars defined with rough content shares
- [ ] Hooks written before body copy
- [ ] Long-form content mined for content atoms
- [ ] Captions stand alone without the source content
- [ ] Calendar covers one to two weeks, with gaps for live posts
- [ ] Daily engagement routine booked
- [ ] Weekly metrics review with the next test chosen
