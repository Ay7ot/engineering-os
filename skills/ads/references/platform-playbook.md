# Paid platform playbook

## Account and campaign structure

```
Account
  Campaign 1: [objective] - [audience or product]
    Ad set 1: [targeting variation]
      Ad 1: [creative A]
      Ad 2: [creative B]
      Ad 3: [creative C]
    Ad set 2: [targeting variation]
  Campaign 2 ...
```

Naming: `[Platform]_[Objective]_[Audience]_[Offer]_[Date]`.

## Budget

Testing phase, first 2-4 weeks: 70 percent to proven campaigns, 30 percent to new audiences and creative.

Scaling: consolidate into winning combinations, raise budgets 20-30 percent at a time, and wait 3-5 days between increases so the algorithm can relearn.

## Retargeting

| Funnel stage | Audience | Message | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Top | Blog readers, video viewers | Educational, social proof | Move to consideration |
| Middle | Pricing and feature page visitors | Case studies, demos | Move to decision |
| Bottom | Cart abandoners, trial users | Urgency, objection handling | Convert |

| Stage | Window | Frequency cap |
|---|---|---|
| Hot, cart or trial | 1-7 days | Higher is acceptable |
| Warm, key pages | 7-30 days | 3-5 times per week |
| Cold, any visit | 30-90 days | 1-2 times per week |

Exclude existing customers unless upselling, recent converters (7-14 days), bounced visitors under 10 seconds, and irrelevant pages such as careers and support.

## Weekly review

- Spend versus budget pacing.
- CPA and ROAS against target.
- Top and bottom performing ads, with the pattern behind each.
- Audience performance breakdown.
- Frequency, and whether fatigue is building.
- Landing page conversion rate.

## Attribution

- Platform attribution is inflated; do not trust it alone.
- Use consistent UTM parameters on every ad.
- Compare platform data with the analytics tool for the same window.
- Judge on blended acquisition cost, not a single platform's CPA.

## Setup before launch

- Install and test conversion tracking with a real conversion.
- Confirm the landing page loads in under 3 seconds and works on mobile.
- Verify UTM parameters arrive correctly.
- Confirm the pixel or event fires on the exact purchase, signup or lead action.
- Check budget, schedule and geo settings.
- Confirm audiences and exclusions match the intent.
