# Lead magnet formats and benchmarks

## Formats

| Type | Best for | Effort | Time to create |
|---|---|---|---|
| Checklist | Quick wins, process steps | Low | 1-2 hours |
| Cheat sheet | Reference material, shortcuts | Low | 2-4 hours |
| Template (document, spreadsheet, workspace) | Repeatable processes | Low to medium | 2-8 hours |
| Swipe file | Inspiration, examples | Medium | 4-8 hours |
| Ebook or guide | Deep education, authority | High | 1-3 weeks |
| Email mini-course | Education plus nurture | Medium | 1-2 weeks |
| Video mini-course | Education plus personality | High | 2-4 weeks |
| Quiz or assessment | Segmentation, engagement | Medium | 1-2 weeks |
| Webinar | Authority, live engagement | Medium | about 1 week of prep |
| Resource library | Ongoing value, return visits | High | ongoing |
| Free trial or community access | Product experience | Varies | Varies |

## Matching format to buyer stage

Awareness, goal is to educate on the problem:

| Format | Example |
|---|---|
| Checklist | "10-Point Website Audit Checklist" |
| Cheat sheet | "SEO Cheat Sheet for Beginners" |
| Ebook or guide | "The Complete Guide to Email Marketing" |
| Quiz | "What Type of Marketer Are You?" |

Consideration, goal is to help evaluate solutions:

| Format | Example |
|---|---|
| Comparison template | "CRM Comparison Spreadsheet" |
| Assessment | "Marketing Maturity Assessment" |
| Case study collection | "5 Companies That Tripled Their Pipeline" |
| Webinar | "How to Choose the Right Analytics Tool" |

Decision, goal is to remove friction to purchase:

| Format | Example |
|---|---|
| Template | "Ready-to-Use Sales Email Templates" |
| Free trial | "14-Day Free Trial" |
| Implementation guide | "Migration Checklist: Switch in 30 Minutes" |
| Calculator | "Calculate Your Savings" |

## Benchmarks

| Metric | Benchmark |
|---|---|
| Landing page conversion, warm traffic | 20-40 percent |
| Landing page conversion, cold traffic | 5-15 percent |
| Lead-to-customer rate | 1-5 percent B2B, wider in other segments |
| Email open rate | 30-50 percent |
| Email click rate | 2-5 percent |

Treat these as sanity checks, not targets. Observe your own baseline before optimising.

## Delivery methods

| Method | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| Instant download | Immediate gratification | No email verification |
| Email delivery | Verifies the address, starts the relationship | Slight delay |
| Thank-you page plus email | Instant access with an email copy | Slightly more setup |
| Drip delivery | Builds a habit over several touchpoints | Only suits courses or series |

## Lead quality check

The magnet attracted good leads when engagement is above average, leads progress to trial or demo at the expected rate, unsubscribes after delivery are low, and most leads match the ideal customer profile. If leads progress poorly, the offer is attracting the wrong stage; change the format or the framing rather than the channel.
