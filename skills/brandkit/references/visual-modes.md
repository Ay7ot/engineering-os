# Visual modes

Choose one mode from the brand category, then keep every panel inside it. Each
mode lists the cues, the logo logic and the intended mood.

## Dark developer or builder
Developer tools, coding agents, infra, automation, AI builders. Near-black panels,
monospace accents, command lines, terminal windows, prompt bars, a subtle grid,
cyan or blue or coral or lime accents, optional pixel or CRT texture. Logo: cursor
plus frame, bolt plus build speed, scaffold plus monogram, modular construction
mark. Mood: precise, sharp, confident.

## Dark product or operator
Business tools, growth tools, sales agents, automation, productivity. Black with
dark red or amber, glowing UI chips, card systems, segmented flows, icon rows,
reward and progress motifs, minimal hero text. Logo: signal, gift, path, operator
mark, switch, loop. Mood: fast, operational, tactical.

## Dark nature or calm system
Strategy, travel, wellness, climate, quiet premium SaaS. Deep green, lime accent,
misty landscapes, image UI circles, soft overlays, calm page labels, dark editorial
grid. Logo: path, leaf, moon, horizon, compass, portal, folded mark. Mood: calm,
trustworthy, focused.

## Dark security or threat intelligence
Security, compliance, monitoring, network products. Black or navy, shield forms,
radar lines, threat labels, motion traces, red and blue alert chips, controlled
gradients. Logo: shield, raptor, eye, watch, boundary, protected core. Mood:
serious, vigilant, precise.

## Light editorial or compliance
Legal, privacy, compliance, documents, trust brands. Warm ivory, paper texture,
small serif labels, seals and badges, a colour wheel or palette object, calm
stationery, deep blue and red and gold accents. Logo: seal, dog, shield, document,
stamp, monogram. Mood: trustworthy, refined, institutional but modern.

## Luxury, beauty or fashion
Beauty, fashion, hospitality, premium services. Ivory, stone, espresso, serif
wordmark, elegant monogram, paper grain, embossing, product labels, editorial
crops, soft shadows. Logo: monogram, seal, petal, vessel, ritual object, refined
typographic mark. Mood: tasteful, adult, expensive.

## Voice or communication
Voice AI, chat, assistants, speech, audio. Dark indigo, lilac glow, waveform, mic
motif, phone crop, command input, app icon. Logo: wave plus initial, sound orb,
speech path, microphone abstraction, pulse ring. Mood: fluid, intelligent,
intimate.

## Cultural or experimental
Music, creative tools, events, gaming-adjacent, cultural products. Halftone, CRT
texture, analog print, a bold accent, poster-style panels, unexpected image crops,
a simple but punchy logo. Logo: custom wordmark, icon with attitude, symbolic
mascot, print-inspired mark. Mood: memorable, creative, still controlled.

## Reference palettes
Black plus cyan plus muted coral; black plus red plus cream plus blue; forest green
plus lime plus fog gray; navy plus white plus steel; ivory plus deep blue plus red
plus gold; black plus lilac plus soft purple; black plus amber plus red; charcoal
plus white plus pale blue. One accent can carry the whole system.

When the user supplies references, extract layout rhythm, grid style, spacing, type
scale, visual density, logo placement, amount of text, image treatment and accent
logic. Do not copy the exact logo, brand name, composition or slogan.
