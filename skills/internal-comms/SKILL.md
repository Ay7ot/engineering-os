# Internal Communications

## When to use
- A recurring update is due: 3P, status report, company newsletter.
- A one-off announcement, leadership note, FAQ, project update or incident report.
- A message must reach several audiences and needs the right format and tone.

Customer-facing or marketing copy is a different job (`copywriting`). Durable incident postmortems and runbooks belong in project docs (`documentation`).

## Procedure
1. Identify the communication type from the request. If it is unclear, ask.
2. Pick the matching format below and fill its sections.
3. Write it where the audience already looks, using the tool the team uses.
4. Proofread for clarity and errors before sending.

## Formats
- **3P update** (Progress, Plans, Problems): weekly team update. Progress is what shipped, Plans is what is next, Problems is blockers and asks.
- **Company newsletter**: executive summary, key achievements, upcoming events, team spotlights.
- **FAQ answer**: the question as asked, a short direct answer, links to resources, who to contact.
- **Status report**: current state, metrics and KPIs, risks with mitigations, next steps.
- **Leadership update**: strategic context, the key decisions, a call to action.
- **Project update**: milestone progress, timeline changes, resource needs, decisions required.
- **Incident report**: timeline of events, impact assessment, root cause, remediation steps.

## Rules
- Know the audience: adjust tone and detail for executives, peers or a whole company.
- Lead with the key message in the first line; do not bury the lede.
- Be concise; respect readers' time.
- Format for scanning: headers, bullets, short paragraphs.
- State next steps and owners. An update with no ask is usually a missed one.
- Separate fact from opinion, and keep incident reports blameless.
- Never put secrets, customer data or unannounced personnel news in a broadcast.

## Writing it
- Open with the single most important fact.
- One idea per paragraph.
- Use plain numbers and units, and say the period they cover.
- Link to detail instead of pasting it.
